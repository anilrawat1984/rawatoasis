A comfortable homestay in Rishikesh

Welcome to Rawat's Oasis, a homestay in Veerbhadra, Rishikesh, for travelers who want room to unwind and an easy base for exploring the city. The home is around a 500-metre walk from a stream fed by the Ganges, with mountain views from the terraces.

Choose a private 1BHK apartment or a studio apartment. The spaces work well for couples, families and small groups, with room for up to seven guests when booking the full accommodation. The host can help you choose the right space and share current availability.

Enjoy a kitchen, air-conditioned bedrooms, Wi-Fi, on-site parking and self check-in. For a budget-friendly stay in Rishikesh, ask us for the current rates for your dates; pricing can vary by season, group size and apartment.

Whether you are planning a quiet couple's break, a family visit or a longer stay near the Ganga, Rawat's Oasis offers the comforts of a private home in a calm part of Rishikesh.

Planning a stay?

Is Rawat's Oasis near the Ganga in Rishikesh?

The homestay is in Veerbhadra, Rishikesh, around a 500-metre walk from a stream fed by the Ganges. Ask the host for directions and travel times to the riverfront and the places you plan to visit.

Do you offer budget-friendly stays in Rishikesh?

There are two private apartment options, a studio and a 1BHK. Rates depend on your dates and accommodation choice, so contact the host for a quote and availability before booking.

Is this a couple-friendly homestay?

Couples are welcome to enquire about either private stay. Please confirm the current guest and ID requirements with the host when making your reservation.

Which amenities are available?

The homestay offers Wi-Fi, a kitchen, air-conditioned bedrooms, on-site parking, private terraces and self check-in. Contact the host if you need details about a particular facility.
